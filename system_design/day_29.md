## Interview Format

### The 45-Minute Structure

- **0-5 min**: Clarify requirements
- **5-10 min**: Estimations
- **10-35 min**: High-level and detailed design
- **35-45 min**: Discuss trade-offs, bottlenecks

### What Interviewers Look For

- Requirements gathering
- Estimation skills
- Clear communication
- Trade-off analysis
- Scalability thinking
