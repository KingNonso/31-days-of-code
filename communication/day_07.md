# Day 7: Week 1 Review - Foundation Integration

## Overview

**Topic**: Integrate all Week 1 skills with comprehensive practice
**Estimated Time**: 60 minutes
**Difficulty**: Intermediate (Checkpoint)

## Prerequisites

- Days 1-6 completed
- All techniques practiced individually
- Recording device ready

## Time Breakdown

- **Review (15 min)**: Recap all techniques
- **Practice (35 min)**: Integrated exercises
- **Reflect (10 min)**: Self-assessment and goals

## Week 1 Techniques Summary

### Day 1: 5 Clarity Mistakes

1. Speaking before thinking
2. Lacking a clear point
3. Excessive filler words
4. Overcomplicating ideas
5. Not reading the room

### Day 2: The Pause

- 3-second rule before responding
- Replace fillers with silence

### Day 3: BLUF

- Bottom Line Up Front
- Lead with conclusion, then support

### Day 4: Rule of Three

- Organize ideas into three points
- Memorable and balanced

### Day 5: Filler Elimination

- Identify personal fillers
- Replace with strategic pauses

### Day 6: Simple Language

- Short sentences, clear words
- No jargon

## Tasks

### Task 1: Full Integration Recording (15 min)

Answer this question using ALL techniques:

**"Tell me about a project you're proud of."**

Checklist while answering:

- [ ] Pause before speaking (Day 2)
- [ ] Lead with conclusion/BLUF (Day 3)
- [ ] Structure with Rule of Three (Day 4)
- [ ] Avoid filler words (Day 5)
- [ ] Use simple language (Day 6)

Record yourself. Listen back. Rate each technique 1-5.

### Task 2: Rapid Fire Practice (15 min)

Answer each question in 30-60 seconds using all techniques:

1. "Why should we hire you?"
2. "What's your biggest weakness?"
3. "Describe a time you failed."
4. "What makes you unique?"
5. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

### Task 3: Written Integration (10 min)

Write a short email (under 100 words) that demonstrates:

- BLUF structure
- Rule of Three
- Simple language
- Clear call to action

Topic: Requesting a meeting with your manager about a new idea.

## Checkpoint Questions

## Common Pitfalls

- **Trying to be perfect**: Integration takes time
- **Forgetting techniques under pressure**: Focus on 1-2 at a time
- **Not recording**: You can't improve what you don't measure
- **Skipping reflection**: Self-assessment drives growth
