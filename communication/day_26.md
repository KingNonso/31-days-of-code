## Cultural Communication Dimensions

### High Context vs Low Context

**High Context** (Japan, Arab countries, Latin America):

- Meaning in context, relationships, nonverbal
- Indirect communication
- "Read between the lines"

**Low Context** (US, Germany, Scandinavia):

- Meaning in words, explicit
- Direct communication
- "Say what you mean"

### Direct vs Indirect Feedback

**Direct** (Netherlands, Israel, Germany):

- Criticism is straightforward
- "The report has errors"

**Indirect** (Japan, UK, Thailand):

- Criticism is softened
- "Perhaps we could review some sections"

### Hierarchy Awareness

**High Power Distance** (China, India, Mexico):

- Respect for authority
- Defer to seniority
- Formal titles important

**Low Power Distance** (Australia, Denmark, US):

- Equality emphasized
- First names common
- Challenge authority is okay

### Time Orientation

**Monochronic** (US, Germany, Switzerland):

- Time is linear, scheduled
- Punctuality matters
- One task at a time

**Polychronic** (Middle East, Latin America):

- Time is flexible
- Relationships > schedules
- Multi-tasking common

## Common Pitfalls

- **Stereotyping**: Individuals vary within cultures
- **Assuming your way is "normal"**: Every style has logic
- **Over-adapting**: Be authentic while respectful
- **Ignoring context**: Culture is one factor among many
