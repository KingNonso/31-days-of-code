## The SBI Model

### What is SBI?

- **S**ituation: When and where did it happen?
- **B**ehavior: What specifically did they do? (Observable, factual)
- **I**mpact: What was the effect? (On you, team, project)

### Why SBI Works

- **Specific**: No vague criticism
- **Objective**: Facts, not judgments
- **Actionable**: Clear what to change

### SBI Example

**Bad Feedback**: "You're always late and it's disrespectful."

**SBI Feedback**:

- **S**: "In yesterday's team meeting..."
- **B**: "...you arrived 15 minutes after it started..."
- **I**: "...which meant we had to repeat the project updates for you, and it delayed our discussion by 10 minutes."

### Positive SBI Too!

- **S**: "During the client presentation last week..."
- **B**: "...you anticipated their objections and addressed them proactively..."
- **I**: "...which helped us close the deal and showed our team's expertise."

## Common Pitfalls

- **Behavior vs. interpretation**: "You were rude" vs "You interrupted three times"
- **Assuming intent**: Focus on behavior, not motivation
- **Sandwich feedback**: SBI is direct, not hidden
- **Too many behaviors**: One SBI per conversation
