## Week 2 Techniques Summary

### Day 8: Active Listening

- HEAR model: Halt, Engage, Anticipate, Reflect
- Listen to understand, not respond

### Day 9: Powerful Questions

- Open vs closed questions
- 5 W's + H framework

### Day 10: STAR Method

- Situation, Task, Action, Result
- 90-120 second responses

### Day 11: Storytelling

- Hooks, stakes, tension, emotion
- Signature story creation

### Day 12: Persuasion

- Ethos (credibility), Pathos (emotion), Logos (logic)
- Balanced persuasion

### Day 13: Handling Objections

- LAER: Listen, Acknowledge, Explore, Respond
- Stay calm, don't get defensive

## Tasks

### Task 1: Full Scenario Practice (20 min)

Role-play this scenario (record yourself):

**Scenario**: You're proposing a new project to your manager who is skeptical.

1. **Open with persuasion** (Ethos, Pathos, Logos)
2. **Use STAR** to give an example of similar success
3. **Handle an objection** with LAER: "We don't have the budget for this."
4. **Ask a powerful question** to understand their concerns
5. **Close with clear next steps**
