## Meeting Communication Principles

### Before the Meeting

- **Purpose**: Why are we meeting? (Decide, discuss, inform?)
- **Agenda**: What will we cover?
- **Pre-work**: What should attendees prepare?
- **Attendees**: Who actually needs to be there?

### During the Meeting

- **Start strong**: State purpose in first 30 seconds
- **Stay focused**: Parking lot for tangents
- **Engage everyone**: Direct questions to quiet people
- **Time-box**: Allocate time per agenda item

### After the Meeting

- **Summary**: Key decisions and actions
- **Owners**: Who does what by when
- **Share**: Send notes within 24 hours

### Participation Best Practices

- Prepare: Review agenda, form opinions
- Contribute: Share perspective, ask questions
- Listen: Build on others' ideas
- Summarize: Clarify decisions before moving on

- Opening (30 sec): State purpose, agenda, time
- Managing discussion: "Let's hear from others..."
- Redirecting tangents: "Great point—let's parking lot that..."
- Closing: "To summarize, we decided... Action items are..."

## Common Pitfalls

- **No agenda**: Meetings without agendas waste time
- **Too many people**: Only invite decision-makers
- **No time-boxing**: Discussions expand to fill time
- **No follow-up**: Decisions lost without notes
