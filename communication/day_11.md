## Storytelling Elements

### The Hook

Start with something that grabs attention:

- **Question**: "Have you ever had to make a decision with $1M on the line?"
- **Surprising fact**: "In my first week, I discovered a bug that had been there for 3 years."
- **Contrast**: "Everyone said it was impossible. I finished it in 2 weeks."

### The Stakes

Make people care by showing what was at risk:

- What happens if you fail?
- Who depends on you?
- What's the worst-case scenario?

### The Tension

Create a "will they/won't they" moment:

- Obstacles faced
- Doubts you had
- Moments of uncertainty

### The Resolution

Satisfying conclusion with:

- Clear outcome
- Lessons learned
- Transformation

### The Emotion

Connect through feelings:

- "I was terrified but..."
- "The relief when it finally worked..."
- "I've never been prouder than when..."

## Common Pitfalls

- **Overembellishing**: Keep it truthful
- **Too much drama**: Subtle tension is effective
- **Forgetting the point**: Story should still answer the question
- **Going too long**: Enhancement shouldn't add time
