## Q&A Strategies

### The PREP Framework

- **P**oint: State your answer directly (BLUF)
- **R**eason: Explain why
- **E**xample: Give concrete illustration
- **P**oint: Restate your answer

### Handling Different Question Types

**Clarification Questions**:
"Great question. To clarify, [direct answer]."

**Challenging Questions**:
"That's an important concern. Here's how I see it..."

**Off-Topic Questions**:
"That's interesting, but perhaps we can discuss that offline..."

**Questions You Don't Know**:
"I don't have that information right now, but I'll follow up with you by [date]."

**Hostile Questions**:
Stay calm. "I understand your concern. Let me address that..."

### Buying Time (Ethically)

- "That's a great question." (Use sparingly)
- Repeat/paraphrase the question
- "Let me think about that for a moment."

### Task 4: "I Don't Know" Practice (10 min)

Practice these responses until they feel natural:

- "I don't have that data, but I'll get it to you by tomorrow."
- "That's outside my expertise. [Colleague name] would be better to answer that."
- "I'd want to research that before giving you an answer. Can I follow up?"
- "I'm not certain, but my understanding is... I'll confirm and let you know."

## Common Pitfalls

- **Rambling answers**: Use PREP for structure
- **Getting defensive**: Questions aren't attacks
- **Faking knowledge**: "I don't know" is better than wrong answers
- **Not repeating the question**: Audience may not have heard it
