## The 5 Clarity-Destroying Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Speaking Before Thinking

- Jumping into responses without organizing thoughts
- Stream-of-consciousness rambling
- **Fix**: Pause, structure, then speak

### Mistake 2: Lacking a Clear Point

- Talking without a main message
- Burying the lead in details
- **Fix**: Start with your conclusion

### Mistake 3: Using Filler Words Excessively

- Um, uh, like, you know, basically
- Undermines credibility and confidence
- **Fix**: Embrace strategic pauses

### Mistake 4: Overcomplicating Simple Ideas

- Using jargon unnecessarily
- Long sentences with multiple clauses
- **Fix**: Simple words, short sentences

### Mistake 5: Not Reading the Room

- Ignoring audience reactions
- One-size-fits-all communication
- **Fix**: Adapt to your audience

### Task 1: Watch the Video (20 min)

Watch the full video, taking notes on:

- Which mistakes resonate most with you?

- Strategies suggested for each mistake

Rate yourself honestly (1-5) on each mistake:

### Task 3: Identify Top 2 Mistakes (10 min)

From your assessment:

1. Which mistake costs you the most credibility?
2. Which mistake is most fixable quickly?

Write a paragraph on each explaining WHY this is a problem for you.

### Task 4: Record Baseline (5 min)

Record yourself explaining your job/role for 60 seconds.

- Don't prepare, just speak naturally
- This is your Day 1 baseline
- You'll compare this to Day 31
