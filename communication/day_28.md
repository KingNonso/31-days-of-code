## Week 4 Techniques Summary

### Day 22: Email Excellence (BRIEF)

- Background, Reason, Information, End, Follow-up
- Clear subject lines, BLUF structure

### Day 23: Chat Communication

- Complete messages, appropriate @mentions
- Async-first mindset

### Day 24: Meeting Communication

- Clear agendas, time-boxing
- Active participation, follow-up notes

### Day 25: Networking

- Give-first mindset
- Follow-up within 24 hours

### Day 26: Cross-Cultural

- High/low context
- Adapt style to culture

### Day 27: Emotional Intelligence

- Self-awareness, empathy
- Respond to feelings, not just words
