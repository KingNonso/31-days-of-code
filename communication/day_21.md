# Day 21: Week 3 Review - Presentation Integration

## Overview

**Topic**: Integrate all presentation and feedback skills
**Estimated Time**: 60 minutes
**Difficulty**: Intermediate (Checkpoint)

## Prerequisites

- Days 15-20 completed
- All Week 3 techniques practiced
- Recording setup ready

## Time Breakdown

- **Review (15 min)**: Recap all techniques
- **Practice (35 min)**: Full presentation with feedback and Q&A
- **Reflect (10 min)**: Comprehensive self-assessment

## Week 3 Techniques Summary

### Day 15: Giving Feedback (SBI)

- Situation, Behavior, Impact
- Specific, observable, actionable

### Day 16: Receiving Feedback (LEARN)

- Listen, Empathize, Ask, Reflect, Navigate
- Move through SARA to Acceptance

### Day 17: Difficult Conversations (STATE)

- Share facts, Tell story, Ask for path, Talk tentatively, Encourage testing

### Day 18: Presentation Structure

- Opening (hook + agenda)
- Body (3 points with support)
- Closing (summary + CTA)

### Day 19: Slide Design

- Less text, more visuals
- 6x6 rule, clear hierarchy

### Day 20: Handling Q&A (PREP)

- Point, Reason, Example, Point
- Handle unknowns gracefully

## Tasks

### Task 1: Full Presentation Delivery (20 min)

Deliver your complete presentation:

- With slides
- 5-7 minutes
- Strong opening and closing
- Record everything

Checklist:

- [ ] Hook grabs attention
- [ ] Three clear points
- [ ] Minimal slide reading
- [ ] Strong call to action

### Task 2: Mock Q&A Session (10 min)

After presentation, have someone ask 3-5 questions:

- Include one challenging question
- Include one you don't know
- Practice PREP responses

### Task 3: Give/Receive Feedback (10 min)

After your presentation:

- Ask someone for feedback on one specific aspect
- Practice LEARN while receiving
- Then give them SBI feedback on something
