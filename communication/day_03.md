## The BLUF Framework

### What is BLUF?

**Bottom Line Up Front**: Start with your conclusion, then provide supporting details.

### The Pyramid Structure

```
        [MAIN POINT]
       /     |      \
   [Support] [Support] [Support]
      |         |         |
  [Detail]  [Detail]  [Detail]
```

### Before vs After BLUF

**Without BLUF (Rambling):**
"So I was looking at the data last week, and there were some interesting trends. The Q2 numbers came in, and we compared them to Q1. There was this spike in March that was unexpected. Anyway, after analyzing everything, I think we should increase the marketing budget."

**With BLUF (Clear):**
"We should increase the marketing budget by 20%. Here's why: Q2 data shows a direct correlation between ad spend and conversions. Specifically, March's campaign drove 3x ROI. Let me show you the details."

### The BLUF Formula

1. **State your recommendation/conclusion**
2. **Provide 2-3 supporting reasons**
3. **Offer details only if asked**

## Common Pitfalls

- **Burying the lead**: Still putting conclusion at the end
- **Too much context first**: Background should support, not lead
- **Fear of being blunt**: BLUF is clear, not rude
- **Forgetting supporting points**: Don't just state - briefly explain why
