## Networking Communication Principles

### The Give-First Mindset

- Lead with value, not asks
- Help before you need help
- Build relationships before you need them

### Conversation Starters

- "What are you working on that excites you?"
- "How did you get into [their field]?"
- "What's the biggest challenge you're facing?"

### Follow-Up Framework

After meeting someone:

1. **Connect within 24 hours** (LinkedIn, email)
2. **Reference something specific** from conversation
3. **Offer value** (article, connection, idea)
4. **Suggest next step** (coffee, call, nothing)

### The 5-5-5 Rule

Each week:

- 5 minutes: Engage with connections' content
- 5 connections: Reach out to new people
- 5 follow-ups: Nurture existing relationships

## Common Pitfalls

- **Asking before giving**: Build relationship first
- **Generic messages**: Personalize every outreach
- **No follow-up**: Most value comes from nurturing
- **Transactional mindset**: Focus on relationships, not transactions
