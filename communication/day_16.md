## The SARA Model (Common Reactions)

When receiving difficult feedback, people often cycle through:

- **S**hock: "What? I had no idea."
- **A**nger: "That's not fair!"
- **R**esistance: "Yes, but..." "They don't understand."
- **A**cceptance: "I can learn from this."

Goal: Move to Acceptance faster.

## The LEARN Framework

### L - Listen Fully

- Don't interrupt
- Take notes
- Control your facial expressions

### E - Empathize with Their View

- They're taking a risk to tell you
- Assume positive intent

### A - Ask Clarifying Questions

- "Can you give me a specific example?"
- "What would improvement look like?"

### R - Reflect and Respond

- "Thank you for sharing this."
- Summarize what you heard

### N - Navigate Next Steps

- "What would you suggest I do differently?"
- Agree on actions

## Common Pitfalls

- **Immediate justification**: "Yes, but..." kills learning
- **Fake acceptance**: Saying thanks but dismissing internally
- **Asking "why"**: Sounds defensive; ask "what" instead
- **Overthinking**: Not all feedback is accurate—but hear it first
