## Powerful Questions Framework

### Open vs Closed Questions

| Closed (Yes/No)    | Open (Expansive)           |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| "Did you like it?" | "What did you think?"      |
| "Is this done?"    | "What's the status?"       |
| "Do you agree?"    | "What's your perspective?" |

### The 5 W's + H

- **What**: "What happened? What do you need?"
- **Why**: "Why is this important? Why now?"
- **Who**: "Who's involved? Who decides?"
- **When**: "When did this start? When is the deadline?"
- **Where**: "Where do you see opportunities?"
- **How**: "How would you approach this?"

### Power Question Starters

- "Tell me more about..."
- "What would happen if..."
- "How do you see..."
- "What's most important about..."
- "What would success look like..."

### Questions to Avoid

- Leading: "Don't you think we should..."
- Loaded: "Why did you make that mistake?"
- Multiple: "What happened and why and who was involved?"

Have a 5-minute conversation where you ONLY ask questions.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Asking "why" defensively**: Use "what led to..." instead
- **Multiple questions at once**: One question at a time
- **Not waiting for answers**: Pause after asking
- **Leading questions**: Let them arrive at conclusions
