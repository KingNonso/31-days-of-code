## Capstone Exercise

### Part 1: Opening Pitch (5 min)

Deliver a 2-minute introduction that includes:

- [ ] Attention-grabbing hook
- [ ] BLUF structure
- [ ] Rule of Three organization
- [ ] Simple, clear language
- [ ] No filler words
- [ ] Confident pauses

Record this.

### Part 2: STAR Story (5 min)

Tell your signature story:

- [ ] Clear Situation
- [ ] Defined Task
- [ ] Specific Actions (what YOU did)
- [ ] Quantified Results
- [ ] Emotional hook
- [ ] Under 2 minutes

Record this.

### Part 3: Persuasive Argument (5 min)

Pitch an idea using Ethos, Pathos, Logos:

- [ ] Establish credibility (Ethos)
- [ ] Present logical argument (Logos)
- [ ] Connect emotionally (Pathos)
- [ ] Clear call to action

Record this.

### Part 4: Handle Objection (5 min)

Respond to: "That's interesting, but we don't have budget for this."

- [ ] Listen/Acknowledge (LA)
- [ ] Explore their concern (E)
- [ ] Respond thoughtfully (R)
- [ ] Stay calm and confident

Record this.

### Part 5: Give and Receive Feedback (5 min)

**Give SBI feedback** on a hypothetical situation:

- [ ] Specific situation
- [ ] Observable behavior
- [ ] Clear impact

**Receive feedback** (have someone critique your capstone):

- [ ] Listen fully
- [ ] Ask clarifying questions
- [ ] Thank them genuinely

### Part 6: Written Communication (5 min)

Write a professional email requesting a meeting:

- [ ] Clear subject line
- [ ] BLUF structure
- [ ] Specific call to action
- [ ] Under 100 words
