## Scenario 1: Job Interview (20 min)

**Setup**: You're interviewing for your dream role.

**Answer these using all techniques:**

1. "Tell me about yourself." (2 min)
   Use: BLUF, Rule of Three, storytelling hook

2. "Tell me about a time you failed." (2 min)
   Use: STAR, emotional honesty, lessons learned

3. "Why should we hire you?" (1 min)
   Use: Ethos, Pathos, Logos

4. "Do you have questions for us?" (1 min)
   Use: Powerful questions

Record all answers.
