## The Three Modes of Persuasion

### Ethos (Credibility)

_Why should they trust you?_

- **Expertise**: Your qualifications, experience
- **Character**: Your integrity, values
- **Goodwill**: Your genuine care for their interests

**Ethos phrases**:

- "In my 10 years of experience..."
- "Having led similar projects..."
- "I understand your concerns because..."

### Pathos (Emotion)

_Why should they care?_

- **Stories**: Personal narratives that connect
- **Vivid language**: Sensory details, imagery
- **Shared values**: Common ground, empathy

**Pathos phrases**:

- "Imagine if you could..."
- "This isn't just about numbers—it's about people..."
- "We've all felt that frustration when..."

### Logos (Logic)

_Why does it make sense?_

- **Data**: Statistics, facts, evidence
- **Structure**: Clear reasoning, cause and effect
- **Examples**: Concrete illustrations

**Logos phrases**:

- "The data shows..."
- "There are three reasons..."
- "For example..."

### The Balance

Most effective persuasion uses all three:

- **Ethos** establishes trust
- **Logos** provides the framework
- **Pathos** motivates action

## Common Pitfalls

- **All logos, no pathos**: Convincing but not motivating
- **All pathos, no logos**: Manipulative, not trustworthy
- **Skipping ethos**: Why should they listen to you?
- **Wrong order**: Establish trust before arguing
