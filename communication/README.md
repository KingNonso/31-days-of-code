## Key Frameworks Covered

### Clarity Frameworks

- **BLUF**: Bottom Line Up Front
- **Rule of Three**: Organize ideas in threes
- **5 Clarity Mistakes**: Common pitfalls to avoid

### Interpersonal Frameworks

- **HEAR**: Halt, Engage, Anticipate, Reflect (Active Listening)
- **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result (Storytelling)
- **Ethos, Pathos, Logos**: Aristotle's Persuasion

### Feedback Frameworks

- **SBI**: Situation, Behavior, Impact (Giving Feedback)
- **LEARN**: Listen, Empathize, Ask, Reflect, Navigate (Receiving)
- **LAER**: Listen, Acknowledge, Explore, Respond (Objections)

### Professional Frameworks

- **STATE**: Share facts, Tell story, Ask for path, Talk tentatively, Encourage testing
- **PREP**: Point, Reason, Example, Point (Q&A)
- **BRIEF**: Background, Reason, Information, End, Follow-up (Email)

## Tips for Success

1. **Record yourself**: You can't improve what you don't measure
2. **Practice out loud**: Silent reading isn't practice
3. **Apply daily**: Use techniques in real conversations
4. **Be patient**: 31 days builds habits, not perfection
5. **Celebrate progress**: Compare Day 31 to Day 1

## FAQ

**Q: What if I miss a day?**
A: Pick up where you left off. Progress over perfection.

**Q: Do I need a practice partner?**
A: Helpful but not required. You can practice solo.

**Q: Will I be a perfect communicator after 31 days?**
A: No, but you'll have frameworks and awareness to keep improving.

**Q: What's the most important thing to practice?**
A: Recording yourself and listening back. It's uncomfortable but transformative.
